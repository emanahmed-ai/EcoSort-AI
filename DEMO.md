## 📝 Video Breakdown

### 1. Problem Statement & Project Significance
* **Video Focus:** The environmental crisis of waste misclassification, associated risks, and the core purpose of EcoSort AI.
* **Key Highlights:**
  - **The Garbage Crisis & Hazards:** Unsorted waste leads to severe environmental pollution, toxic landfill emissions, resource wastage, and health hazards for sanitation workers.
  - **Real-World Challenges:** Traditional sorting methods fail under real-world conditions like dirt, severe occlusion, damaged items, and overlapping objects.
  - **Project Importance:** EcoSort AI bridges the gap between controlled academic datasets and practical waste management by delivering robust, automated material identification.
  - **Core Achievement:** Achieved **96.33% accuracy** on a strict, leak-free test dataset across 10 target waste categories.

### 2. Deployment Mockup & Customization Potential
* **Video Focus:** Conceptual demonstration of the user interface and system workflow.
* **Key Highlights:**
  - **Proof of Concept:** Demonstrates real-time image capture, inference speed, and classification logic in a simulated environment.
  - **Adaptable & Client-Ready:** This mockup represents a foundational prototype; the interface, model pipeline, and integration constraints can be fully customized and tailored to meet the specific requirements of any client, municipal facility, or enterprise stakeholder.
  - **Safety Features:** Built-in low-confidence score flagging to route ambiguous items safely for manual review.
